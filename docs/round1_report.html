1. Idea & Approach: Autonomous Discovery vs. Simple Summarisation

Conventional financial AI agents function merely as document summarizers: they process press releases and repeat management's optimistic talking points verbatim. In the Indian FMCG sector (Track C), critical inflection points are rarely acknowledged directly. A firm routinely highlights "10% revenue expansion" and "premiumization" in its headline investor presentations while burying negative underlying volume growth (-1.5%) in secondary footnotes and masking a 65% surge in distributor credit days inside statutory balance sheet notes.

Core Approach: Rather than summarizing text, Signal-C executes deterministic forensic hypothesis testing across three vectors: (1) Price vs Volume Decoupling (decomposing revenue into Underlying Volume Growth [UVG] vs price realization), (2) Channel Working Capital Strain (tracking Days Sales Outstanding [DSO] to catch distributor channel stuffing), and (3) Margin Mirage Detection (reconciling Gross Margin expansion against Advertising & Sales Promotion [A&P] budget cuts).

2. System Architecture & Tools Stack

  • Orchestration & State Machine: Python asynchronous multi-agent pipeline composed of 6 specialized agent personas: ScoutAgent (ingestion), ForensicAnalystAgent (ratio engine), PatternDetectionAgent (contradiction audit), HypothesisGeneratorAgent (thesis formulation), DevilsAdvocateAgent (self-falsification), and AuditSynthesisAgent (dossier compilation).
  • Reasoning & Validation: High-precision LLM reasoning with strict Pydantic validation schemas preventing hallucinations and enforcing zero ungrounded claims.
  • Data Retrieval (Public Internet): Automated adapters querying BSE/NSE statutory corporate filings, investor presentations, audited annual report schedules, earnings call transcripts, and All India Consumer Products Distributors Federation (AICPDF) channel bulletins.
  • Deterministic Financial Calculators: Python numerical modules computing UVG, DSO trends, Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC), and Operating Cash Flow (OCF)-to-EBITDA conversion.
  • Delivery Interfaces: High-performance FastAPI server delivering both an interactive real-time research dashboard with Chart.js visualizations and a publication-ready equity research report.

3. Autonomous Workflow: From Ticker to Audited Discovery

Given any NIFTY 50 FMCG ticker, the agent progresses through an auditable six-stage lifecycle:

  1. Discover: ScoutAgent identifies peer groupings and ingests trailing 8 quarters of financial statements, balance sheet notes, and earnings calls.
  2. Calculate: ForensicAnalystAgent derives volume-price contribution shares, computes debtor days DSO, and measures operating cash flow conversion.
  3. Connect & Triangulate: Reconciles management narrative optimism against statutory notes to accounts (Schedule III Trade Receivables) and peer benchmarks.
  4. Reason: Forms the core discovery thesis highlighting the non-obvious divergence between reported growth and economic reality.
  5. Challenge (Self-Falsification): DevilsAdvocateAgent actively searches for sector-wide explanations (e.g. general raw material inflation, quick-commerce terms) and derives mathematically falsifiable test conditions.
  6. Prove: Formulates the auditable 8-point research dossier, mapping every claim to primary source citations.

4. Track C Generalisation Without Company-Specific Tuning

The system achieves 100% zero-shot generalisation across all NIFTY 50 FMCG companies (Britannia, HUL, Tata Consumer, Nestle India, ITC) and any non-NIFTY 50 listed peer (Dabur, Marico, Godrej Consumer) because its analytical vectors are rooted in the fundamental unit economics of consumer goods: Reported Revenue Growth ≡ UVG + Price Realization. Furthermore, standardized Schedule III Balance Sheets and Ind AS 115 revenue disclosures ensure universal parsing compatibility across all Indian listed companies.

5. Triangulation, Contradiction Detection & Self-Falsification

  • Triangulation: Every finding must be corroborated across at least 4 independent primary sources (Investor Presentation + Balance Sheet Note + Concall Transcript + Competitor Filing).
  • Contradiction Detection: Detects narrative divergence (e.g., management claiming "rural consumption acceleration" while distributor receivables expand by 14 days and OCF-to-EBITDA drops below 70%).
  • Self-Falsification (Devil's Advocate): Formulates rigorous, quantitative conditions that would disprove the thesis (e.g. "Falsified if the company logs two consecutive quarters of UVG > +3.5% with DSO normalizing below 26 days") and rates Evidence Strength as Strong or Moderate with auditable rationale.

6. Required 8-Point Output & Delivery Format

The output delivers all 8 mandatory elements: The Discovery (1 clear sentence), Why It Matters (3–5 bullets), Evidence Trail (multi-source clickable citations), Timeline (chronological emergence), Numbers (exact calculated tables and charts), Counterargument (strongest alternative thesis), Falsification Condition (future test), and Evidence Strength (with justification). Output is presented as an interactive web dashboard and a printable institutional equity note.
